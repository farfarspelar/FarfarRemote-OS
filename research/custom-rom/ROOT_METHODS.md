## Recommendation

There are three distinct research leads, none currently safe to execute:

1. **KingoRoot/KingRoot/iRoot exploit routes:** community reports include claims for `.2.31` and a downgrade-to-`.1.28` then OTA workflow. Reports conflict on whether OTA verification succeeds and root survives. Exact successful handset variant/CDA, binaries, hashes and clean-stock reproduction are missing. The downgrade workflow also requires flashing firmware. These reports raise historical plausibility only; they do not provide a safe, repeatable current procedure. Do not install or execute the opaque tools or start a downgrade/OTA sequence.
2. **Custom recovery route:** an E2003-specific historical TWRP artifact exists for static review. Device/build-family identity, kernel match and partition mapping are useful evidence; source, build provenance, R7A compatibility and rollback safety remain unverified. Do not test it until an exact/official restore route is independently established and separate authorization is given.
3. **Magisk patch route:** technically cleaner and auditable if we obtain the exact R7A boot image and can convert Sony's ELF representation without changing boot semantics. The local candidate is R10A and no standard `boot.img` or verified restore route exists.

So the answer is: root is **plausible but not yet demonstrated on clean stock for this exact CDA/build**. The weak KingRoot report and historical E4g recovery reports justify continued research, not execution. Do not execute KingRoot/KingoRoot or proprietary PC tools. Do not unlock, flash, wipe, disable system packages, or write any partition in this phase.

## Minimum evidence before a future root attempt

1. Verify exact device identity, build fingerprint and customization again.
2. Acquire stock firmware from a source with traceable provenance; verify archive integrity and obtain the original boot payload.
3. Confirm exact image is for E2003/25.0.A.2.31 and inspect header/ramdisk locally.
4. Confirm the Service Menu explicitly reports `Bootloader unlocked: Yes` if unlock was performed, and establish a model-appropriate stock restore route independently. Eligibility (`Bootloader unlock allowed: Yes`) is not sufficient.
5. If considering the historical KingRoot lead, first identify the exact APK/version referenced and establish provenance, hash, behavior and exploit side effects in an isolated analysis environment. If that cannot be done, discard this route and continue with an auditable boot-image method.
6. Rehearse a read-only/dry-run recovery procedure. Document likely data loss and preserve FarfarRemote settings.
7. Treat unlocking as a separate irreversible operation that factory-resets data and prevents restoration of Sony DRM keys; obtain separate explicit authorization before any unlock, root, flash or other partition-write operation.
